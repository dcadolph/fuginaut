A second clock at the center of your menu bar.

Apple's clock sits in the far right corner of the screen. On a wide monitor, that's a long way from whatever you're working on. Fuginaut draws a second clock in the middle of the menu bar on every display, where a glance finds it.

Color

Make it yours.

Every change shows live as you make it. The docs cover every option.

Size and position

Right where you look.

The center of the menu bar on every display, from 9 to 40 points, with a nudge if something already lives there.

Text

Any font on your Mac.

System, Rounded, Mono, Serif, or any family you have installed, in four weights.

Color

Match the bar, or don't.

24 swatches, a hex value, or the color wheel, with a shadow or pill to keep it readable.

Format

Sixteen date styles, or your own.

Weekday, date, and time in either order, 12 or 24 hour, with text or emoji around the clock.

World

Your language, numerals, and calendar.

73 languages, 22 sets of numerals, and 24 calendars, mixed freely, plus a second time zone.

General

It stays out of your way.

Click the eclipse to turn it on or off. Pick which displays show it, even in full screen apps.

Six looks. One moment, seven ways.

No permissions. Nothing collected.

Fuginaut asks for no permissions and has no account. No version makes network requests. Read the privacy policy.
